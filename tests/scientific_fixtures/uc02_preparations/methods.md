# Materials and Methods

## Cell culture
Primary cortical neurons were prepared from three independent preparations. Each preparation was plated into four wells.

## Treatment
Cells were treated with NGF or vehicle at the level of the culture preparation for 24 h.

## Quantitative microscopy
TH immunofluorescence was quantified as CTCF per cell. Five fields were acquired per well.

## Statistical analysis
Groups were compared with an unpaired t-test; n = 120 cells per group.
