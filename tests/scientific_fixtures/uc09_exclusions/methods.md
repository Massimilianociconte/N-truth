# Materials and Methods

## Animals
The study used ten animals. Animals were treated with drug or vehicle at the level of the animal. Five animals were allocated per group.

## Endpoint-specific analysis
Cell viability per animal was measured.

For the cell viability endpoint, analyzed n = 5 animals in the drug group.

For the cell viability endpoint, analyzed n = 4 animals in the vehicle group after one animal was excluded because of technical failure.

Marker intensity per animal was measured.

For the marker intensity endpoint, analyzed n = 4 animals in the drug group after one animal was excluded because of technical failure.

For the marker intensity endpoint, analyzed n = 5 animals in the vehicle group.

## Statistical analysis
Each endpoint was compared between groups with an unpaired t-test.
