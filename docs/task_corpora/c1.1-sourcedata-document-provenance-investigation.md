## Executive result

```text
PRIMARY_STATUS: RECOVERABLE_PARTIAL_DETERMINISTIC
```

An official upstream asset (`xml/source_data_xml_v2.0.3.tar.gz` on the official
Hugging Face dataset `EMBO/SourceData`) carries full article/figure/panel
identifiers. A conservative deterministic join assigns provenance to
**74,072 / 75,163 (98.55%)** of locked SourceData records with
**zero articles and zero panels crossing official splits** among matched
records. **1,091 records (1.45%) remain unmatched** and stay
`RECORD_FALLBACK`. Because recovery is not total and because the locked
upstream revision is no longer resolvable, the result is **partial**, not full.

No canonical manifest, JSONL, leakage audit, partition or readiness status was
modified by this investigation.

## 3. Join feasibility (Phase C)

Strategies evaluated in the mandated order:

| # | Strategy | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Explicit stable identifier join | Impossible — locked export carries no identifiers |
| 2 | Official source-record ID join | Impossible — upstream JSONL rows carry no row IDs |
| 3 | Exact canonical text join | Measured below |
| 4 | Exact tuple with unique upstream occurrence | Subsumed by S3 uniqueness rule |
| 5 | Conservative deterministic composite (exact + verbatim containment, DOI-collapsed) | Measured below |

Normalization: whitespace-collapse only, applied identically on both sides;
original text retained; no fuzzy similarity; no first-match behaviour;
ambiguous records remain unmatched.

normalized captions** (1,953 duplicated caption keys upstream).

### Unmatched residual (1,091 records)

Sampled unmatched records are multi-sentence figure-legend segments produced
by upstream *panelization* (concatenated sentence segments with panel-start
markers), e.g. leading "G/H …" sentence fragments of one figure legend. They
do not occur verbatim in any single panel caption and cannot be assigned
without a panelization-aware reconstruction — which is a **migration
operation**, not an investigation step, and was not authorised here.

## 5. Leakage implications

- For the 74,072 matched records, leakage grouping can be elevated to
  **ARTICLE** (DOI) with PANEL refinement for the 70,158 unique tier-1
  matches — **if and only if** a separately authorised migration lands.
- Under the matched-only mapping, **zero** article or panel groups cross the
  official upstream splits.
- The 1,091 unmatched records remain `RECORD_LEVEL_FALLBACK`; therefore
  `paper_level_leakage_claim_allowed` must remain `false` and
  `document_level_leakage` cannot become VERIFIED from this investigation
  alone.
- Official upstream splits were used (`split_authority: upstream_official`);
  no N-Truth partition was approved or changed.

## 6. Limitations

1. Locked revision `b457c140…` is no longer resolvable upstream; provenance
   continuity rests on byte-identical content of all six locked files at
   current `main` plus identical version tag `v2.0.3`.
2. The XML archive exists only at current `main`; its pin cannot be expressed
   against the locked revision. Its SHA-256 is recorded here and in the YAML
   companion.
3. Containment evidence was restricted to verbatim substring occurrence with
   DOI-collapsed assignment; segment→panel reconstruction was deliberately
   excluded.
4. Licence scope for training/evaluation remains unresolved
   (`PENDING_LICENSE_SCOPE_CLOSURE`); this investigation does not change it.
5. `split_files/split_v2.0.2.json` is the only published split file and is
   v2.0.2; split identity for v2.0.3 rests on the locked file paths used at
   acquisition.

## 7. Recommended next action

A **separately authorised** deterministic migration (Workstream B re-lock
process): panelization-aware segment reconstruction against the official XML
v2.0.3 corpus, new Merkle revision, updated leakage grouping with explicit
`DOCUMENT_ID`/`MIXED` granularity reporting, and re-validation of
`records_sha256` inputs. Until then: no manifest, readiness or leakage status
changes.
