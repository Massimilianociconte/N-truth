# C1.1 investigation erratum — unique-unit granularity decomposition (2026-08-06)

Status: ADDITIVE ERRATUM. The merged C1.1 investigation document
(`c1.1-sourcedata-document-provenance-investigation.md`) is NOT rewritten;
this erratum refines one reported figure.

## What changes

The C1.1 investigation reported a combined unique-unit count of **70,158**
under PANEL granularity. That population is decomposed here into two distinct
official granularities:

| Granularity | Records |
|---|---|
| panel_unique (authoritative `sd-panel` units) | 69,983 |
| figure_unique (whole-figure units, no `sd-panel` child) | 175 |
| combined unique upstream units (unchanged) | 70,158 |

## Reason

175 authoritative whole-figure XML units have **no `sd-panel` child** and
therefore cannot truthfully be called panel-level provenance. They remain
deterministic, unique, exact assignments at FIGURE granularity; no panel
identifier is invented for them.

## Coverage (unchanged)

- unique upstream units: **70,158** (sum of the two granularities)
- matched any granularity: **74,072** (98.548% of 75,163)
- fallback (RECORD_FALLBACK): **1,091**

All other C1.1 findings, counters, fail-closed rules and limitations stand.
The refined vocabulary is carried by sidecar schema 0.2.0
(`TIER_1_PANEL_UNIQUE` / `TIER_1_FIGURE_UNIQUE` / `TIER_2_ARTICLE_ONLY` /
`RECORD_FALLBACK`).
